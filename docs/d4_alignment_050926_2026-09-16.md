`tests/test_rejected_transaction_defaults.py::test_sigma_tables_internal_consistency_and_the_two_doc_slips`

decision.**

high**. The five RT quintile entries *are* consistent with the factor 0.332208167. Either the
overall value should read 0.39411104, or the intended factor is different.

row (0.4665145336) agrees with it. But all five per-allowance entries imply a factor of

multiplied once more by **1.0228235**, which is the *level-1* mean of `stdactions`:
